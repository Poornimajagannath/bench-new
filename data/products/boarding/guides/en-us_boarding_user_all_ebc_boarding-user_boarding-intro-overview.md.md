Introduction to the Boarding Registration Service {#boarding-intro-overview}
============================================================================

Boarding Registration streamlines and automates the boarding of merchant accounts using the `Cybersource` `Business Center`. You can create a simple or complex hierarchy of accounts that represents merchant business units, and configure payment products for those accounts. To understand accounts, organizations, their hierarchy, and status, see these topics:

* [Understanding Accounts and Organizations](/docs/cybs/en-us/boarding/user/all/ebc/boarding-user/boarding-intro-overview/boarding-intro-organizations.md "")
* [Extend the Hierarchy](/docs/cybs/en-us/boarding/user/all/ebc/boarding-user/boarding-extend-hierarchy.md "")
* [Change an Organization's Status](/docs/cybs/en-us/boarding/user/all/ebc/boarding-user/merchants-v2-status-change.md "")
